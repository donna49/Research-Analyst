## Trigger (stage 2)

Before the open on 2026-10-16, a press release (filed later that day as 8-K Exhibit 99.1,
accepted 16:06 ET) announced three things at once (E023–E024; filing time E025, release-time gap E026):
1. Preliminary Q3 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $38–42M against guidance of $46–54M; net sales
   $336–340M against $355–380M.
2. Fiscal 2026 adjusted EBITDA outlook cut to $140–150M from $176–196M; net sales to
   $1.35–1.38B from $1.42–1.47B.
3. CEO Michael Dennison stepping down; director Douglas Grimm interim CEO.

Reasons the company gave: Marucci Sports "did not deliver to expectations" before its
2026-09-25 sale; a supply disruption and associated higher costs in Powered Vehicles; continued
operating challenges in the upfit business and lower chassis availability (Aftermarket);
softer bike sales; higher steel, aluminum and freight inflation.

Market and sector moves explain almost none of the fall (E027). The CEO change and the guidance
cut came in one release, so their separate effects cannot be split from daily prices (E028).
