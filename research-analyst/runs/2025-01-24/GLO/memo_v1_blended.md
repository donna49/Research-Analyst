## OVERVIEW

Global Atomic Corporation ("GLO" or the "Company"; CUSIP 37957M106) is an Ontario corporation, incorporated under the *Business Corporations Act* (Ontario) on May 27, 1994, with its registered and principal office at 8 King Street East, Suite 1700, Toronto [E103][E105]. It describes itself as:

> "Global Atomic is a Toronto-based company that provides a unique combination of cash-flowing zinc concentrate production and high-grade uranium development." [E106]

- **Listings.** Common shares on the TSX ("GLO"), the OTCQX ("GLATF") and Frankfurt ("G12"); a reporting issuer in all provinces and territories of Canada [E104].
- **Shares outstanding.** 202,253,352 (August 10, 2023), 263,393,073 (November 12, 2024) and 308,068,073 (March 27, 2025) [E114][E115][E116]. Market capitalization at the start of the Class Period: C$407.3M [E190].
- **Key asset.** The Dasa uranium project in Niger, held by SOMIDA (Société Minière de DASA S.A.), owned 80% by the Company and 20% by the Republic of Niger [E107]. The Company also holds a 49% interest in a Turkish zinc-recycling joint venture with Befesa [E106].
- **Insiders.** Stephen G. Roman, Chairman, President & CEO (director since 2005); Rein A. Lehari, CFO; directors and officers held 21,239,768 shares, about 7% [E108][E109][E113].

**The theory in one paragraph.** From November 10, 2023 the Company told the market that the U.S. Government supported its project financing, that a U.S. development bank (the U.S. International Development Finance Corporation, "DFC") had been authorized to re-engage and finalize funding arrangements, and that the bank's Credit Committee and Board would approve a US$295M facility on a schedule that moved every quarter: January/March 2024, Q2 2024, April/June 2024, mid-July/September 2024, August/October 2024, Q3 or Q4 2024, early Q1 2025 [E037][E038][E046][E049][E051][E052][E055][E057][E058][E060]. In November 2024 it said Dasa was "in the final stages" of approval and that it had "answered all geo-political concerns raised by the bank" [E061][E062]. On January 23, 2025, after the close, three weeks into the quarter in which approval had been promised, the Company raised equity at a discount and said the "major component of funding" would come as "either a bank financing or a joint venture agreement" [E028][E029]. The market's reaction was harsh and immediate: −31.9% on 13.6× volume, an abnormal return of −31.3% (t −10.2), and no recovery over the ten-day look-back [E176][E008][E009][E011]. The Company later disclosed a "pause before and after the 2024 U.S. presidential election" and that the loan first reached the bank's Credit Committee in about December 2025 [E071][E073]; DFC's own 2024 board records list no Dasa approval [E076].

**Constituent elements.**
- OSA s. 138.3: "(a) a responsible issuer, or an individual acting with its actual or apparent authority, disseminates a document that includes a misrepresentation; (b) the investor acquired the security after the document's issuance but prior to the public correction of the misrepresentation; and (c) the claimant possesses a right of action for damages against the issuer and its directors, among other statutory defendants." *Stajic v. Ward*, 2026 ONSC 1707, at para. 15 [E200].

Hycroft Mining Corp*, 2020 ONSC 59, at para. 20; *Stajic v. Ward*, 2026 ONSC 1707, at para. 13 [E201].
