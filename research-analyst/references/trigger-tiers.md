# Trigger tiers

Used by triage and trigger-finder to rank what moved the stock. Tiers measure how often a trigger of
this kind turns out to involve earlier statements on the same subject; they are not findings.

## Tier A: high signal

| Trigger | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Restatement, non-reliance on prior financials (US 8-K Item 4.02) | Earlier numbers admitted wrong |
| Auditor resignation, dismissal or qualified/adverse opinion (8-K Item 4.01) | Auditor disagreement or withdrawal |
| Material weakness in internal controls | Reliability of reported figures in question |
| Delayed periodic filing (NT 10-K / NT 10-Q; late annual report abroad) | Often precedes restatement |
| Regulator or prosecutor action: investigation, subpoena, Wells notice, raid, enforcement | Third party examining conduct |
| Short-seller report alleging misconduct or misstatement | Specific allegations to test |
| Court judgment or ruling on a matter the company had downplayed | Outcome vs earlier assessment |
| Guidance withdrawn or cut soon after being reaffirmed | Timing of knowledge |
| Sudden CEO or CFO departure, especially "effective immediately" | Often linked to the issue |
| Going-concern doubt; covenant breach; emergency financing | Earlier liquidity statements |
| Clinical trial, regulatory or product failure after confident statements | Gap between tone and data |
| Dividend cut or suspension after assurances | Earlier sustainability statements |
| Write-down of an asset or acquisition recently promoted | Valuation statements |
| Operational disruption described earlier as contained or immaterial | Direct contradiction risk |
| Loss of a key customer, contract or licence the company had presented as secure | Concentration statements |
| Data breach, recall or safety issue disclosed late | Disclosure timing |

## Tier B: medium

| Trigger | Note |
|---|---|
| Earnings miss with guidance cut, no recent reaffirmation | Common business disappointment; check what was said in the prior quarter |
| Analyst downgrade citing new facts (not just valuation) | Find the underlying fact |
| Discounted share sale with an unexplained or changed use of proceeds | Check prior liquidity statements |
| Partner or collaborator ending a relationship | Check how the relationship was described |

## Tier C: low

| Trigger | Note |
|---|---|
| Macro, rates, commodity or crypto price move | Group move; usually excluded by the drop standard |
| Sector news not naming the company | Excluded unless the company is singled out |
| Acquirer falling on its own deal announcement | Price of the deal, not concealed facts |
| Index deletion, lock-up expiry, technical flows | Mechanical |
| Valuation-only downgrade | Opinion on price |

## Unexplained

No identifiable news by day 0 close. Goes to Watch with a re-scan in 5 trading days; the cause often
surfaces in a later filing, a lawsuit or reporting.
