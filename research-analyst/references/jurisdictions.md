# Jurisdiction notes

Context for what to collect, by where the company is listed. This is a research aid, not legal
advice; the lawyer decides which regime applies. Record facts (incorporation, listings, ADRs,
dates); do not conclude which law governs.

| Listing | Disclosure documents to collect | Timing questions to answer | Facts to record |
|---|---|---|---|
| **US** (NYSE, Nasdaq, NYSE American; OTC noted separately) | 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and exhibits, proxy, earnings calls, press releases, S-1/S-3 if shares were sold | When each statement was made; when the corrective information was public (to the minute when same-day) | Exchange; whether shares trade on a national exchange or OTC; insider Form 4 trades in the period |
| **Canada** (TSX, TSXV, CSE, Cboe Canada) | Annual information form, MD&A, financial statements, material change reports, news releases, earnings calls | When a material change occurred vs when it was reported | Provinces where reporting issuer; any US listing (then also US documents) |
| **UK** (LSE Main Market, AIM) | Annual and half-year reports, RNS announcements (trading updates, inside information), prospectuses | When inside information arose vs when it was announced; any delayed-disclosure notices | Main Market or AIM; any US listing or ADR |
| **Germany** (Xetra / Frankfurt regulated market) | Annual and half-year reports, ad hoc announcements, corporate news, quarterly statements | When the underlying event occurred vs when the ad hoc announcement was published; any self-exemption delay later disclosed | Market segment; any US listing or ADR |

For any company with a US listing or ADR, collect the US documents too, whatever its home country.
For companies quoted only on US OTC markets but listed at home (e.g. a Japanese company on OTC),
record the home listing; the home market price is the meaningful one.
