# Andrew's memo corpus: exemplars by case type

Use this index at stage 3, once the case type is known (`case-types.md`). Read the one or two
closest exemplars with the Google Drive connector (`read_file_content` with the file ID below)
before drafting the claim framing and the memo. Exemplars teach **method and voice only**. Never
copy their facts, names, tickers or numbers. Several of his own memos contain paragraphs carried
over from other matters, and `ledger.py memo` fails if a foreign ticker appears in the memo.

Quality grades:
- **A**: complete, current form; the best model.
- **B**: solid, but an older form or partly drafted.
- **C**: method only; do not use as a model of the text, because the memo is unfinished or contains
  carried-over text.

The full inventory (179 memo files) is in the Drive inventory of 2026-10-18 (`drive_inventory_full.csv`).
