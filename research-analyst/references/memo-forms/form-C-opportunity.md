Causes of Action

A. Common law misrepresentations. This cause of action would be limited to Canadian based investors; the elements of a claim of negligent misrepresentation are: (1) duty of care based on a special relationship between the plaintiff and the defendant; (2) an untrue, inaccurate, or misleading representation; (3) the defendant making the representation negligently; (4) the plaintiff having reasonably relied on the misrepresentation; and, (5) the plaintiff suffering damages as a consequence of relying on the misrepresentation.

B. Section 138.3 of the Securities Act, if leave is granted. The constituent elements of the statutory cause of action are: (1) the making of a misrepresentation or the failure to disclose a material fact; (2) a public correction of the false information; and (3) the trading of a security between the time of the misrepresentation or the failure to disclose a material fact. Thus, to plead the statutory causes of action, the plaintiff should: (1) identify the inculpatory statement or omission and when it was made or ought to have been made; (2) specify the falseness of the inculpatory statement; and (3) identify the public correction (which content must be "linked" to the alleged misrepresentations) and when it was made.
