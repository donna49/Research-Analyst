Summary of the December 4, 2024, DFC Board of Directors Meeting
The DFC Board of Directors met at 2:30 PM, on Wednesday, December 4, 2024. The meeting was
preceded by a public hearing session from 2:00 pm – 2:30 pm where the Board and DFC Management
heard from six presenters representing various organizations. In attendance at the Board of Directors
meeting were: Deputy Secretary of State for Management and Resources Richard Verma, USAID
Acting Deputy Administrator Michelle Sumilas, DFC Chief Executive Officer Scott Nathan, Deputy
Secretary of Commerce Don Graves, Mr. Chris Vincze, Mr. Irving Bailey, and Mr. Deven Parekh.
During the December 4, 2024 meeting, the Board heard opening remarks from Deputy Secretary of
Management and Resources Richard Verma, Acting Deputy Administrator Michele Sumilas, and Chief
Executive Officer Scott Nathan. The Board approved two transactions, Banco Rio S.A.E.C.A. and
Frontier Clearing Corp, totaling $170 Million. In addition, the Board received a preview of transactions
that are expected to be considered at the January meeting of the Board, a report of the meeting of the
Board’s Audit Committee held on December 3, 2024, and an update on Clean Energy Supply Chain
matters. The Board also was briefed on the DFC sector strategies for the Political Risk Insurance and the
Small Business and Financial Services business lines. The Board then, based on the Framework for
Assessing Dividends approved by the Board on December 7, 2022, assessed that no dividend should be
paid from FY2024 results. The Board then heard from the Director of DFC’s Independent
Accountability Mechanism (IAM) and approved the Terms of Reference for the DFC IAM. The Board
further approved the appointment of certain corporate officers. The resolutions adopted for the approved
projects and these other matters are available for review at https://www.dfc.gov/who-we-are-our-peopleboard-directors/board-resolutions. Following the General Session, the Board held an Executive Session
and then adjourned at approximately 4:30 PM.
