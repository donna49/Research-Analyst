Global Atomic Corporation (“Global Atomic”) through its wholly owned subsidiary Global Atomic Fuels
Corporation owns an 80% interest in SOMIDA S.A. a Nigerein Company; the Government of Niger owns
the remaining 20% of SOMIDA. SOMIDA owns the DASA Uranium Project located in Niger, Africa.
The DASA Uranium Project is fully permitted for underground mining and construction of supporting
infrastructure. Underground mine development and construction of surface infrastructure are currently
underway, commercial production is scheduled to begin in early 2025.
